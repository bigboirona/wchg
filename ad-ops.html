WC Holdings Ad Boss

A private, operator-directed system for authorized advertising-account reporting, creative planning, experimentation, and separately supervised creation of draft, paused, or disabled campaign objects.

Who uses it

WC Holdings Group uses Ad Boss for its own legitimate brands and for advertisers that have expressly authorized the relevant account. It is not a public consumer login, identity broker, or account-resale service.

What it reads

With administrator consent, the integration reads the exact authorized advertiser inventory and limited campaign, creative, conversion-configuration, delivery, and performance data needed for reporting and analysis.

What it can prepare

The private system can prepare campaign briefs, channel-native creative, measurement plans, A/B tests, and reviewable provider requests. Reporting-only authorization is established before any separate write eligibility is considered.

Safety boundary

Any supported write is separately owner-approved and limited to a reviewed draft, paused, or disabled object. OAuth consent alone does not authorize activation, public delivery, live-budget mutation, automatic retry, payment, or advertising spend.

Account separation

Each advertiser keeps a distinct identity, account hierarchy, destination, policy evidence, creative lineage, budget ceiling, and reporting history. The system does not reuse one advertiser's authority or data for another advertiser.

Control your data

Account administrators can revoke access in the provider's settings or request disconnection and eligible data deletion through the contact below. Security, legal, and compliance audit records may be retained where necessary.
