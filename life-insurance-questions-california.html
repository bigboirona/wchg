The protection checkup

Three useful questions to answer first

1

Who depends on your income?

Consider children, a spouse or partner, debts, housing, education goals, caregiving, and other responsibilities.

2

What coverage already exists?

Workplace coverage can be a useful starting point. Ask how much it provides, whether it follows you, and how it fits the complete need.

3

How long is protection needed?

Different obligations have different time horizons. Understanding those dates helps organize the questions before policy comparisons begin.

General education

Term and permanent coverage can address different needs.
