Understand the limitations

The assumptions matter.

IUL is life insurance, not a direct investment in an index. Non-guaranteed values, crediting and policy performance can change with policy costs, caps, participation rates, loan rates and ongoing funding.

Policy loans, leverage and premium financing add repayment, interest-rate and collateral risks. An underfunded or unmonitored policy may not perform as illustrated.

Review carrier illustrations with appropriate tax, legal and financial professionals in the context of your broader situation.
